For years I've worked around software without building it myself — deploying experiments, debugging production issues, writing JSON and SQL, sitting next to the engineers who were actually shipping things. This blog is where I write about going deeper: what I'm learning, what's confusing, what finally clicked.

It won't only be code. I'm just as interested in the other things I'm building skill in outside of work — cooking, coffee and latte art, guitar — and in how learning itself works, since that thread runs through all of it.

A few things to expect:

- Notes on what I'm learning in software engineering — data structures, system design, whatever I'm stuck on that week
- Coffee and latte art, since I'm slowly getting less bad at it
- Cooking experiments, guitar practice, and whatever else I'm deliberately getting better at
- Occasional reflections on how learning actually works, since I find that as interesting as the skills themselves

No fixed schedule. I'll post when I have something worth writing down.
